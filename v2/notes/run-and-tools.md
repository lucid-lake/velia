URL contract, the same as v1's: `?hour=&cam=&seed=&exposure=&t=&day=&headless=1`, plus v1's wind rows `wind=1–4`,
`windspeed=` (m/s), `winddir=` (compass, where from). **M2: `cam` is v1's preset** (`src/sim/core/presets.js`: A above the
bay by the mouth, B a boat's eye at 1.6 m, I by Fannette Island, O the overlook, L and M out on the lake; A when absent),
so a v1 and a v2 capture of the same URL share the pose; `campos=x,y,z&camlook=x,y,z` any viewpoint; `camyaw`/`campitch`
turn the preset's eye (a yaw alone looks 2° up, M1's framing); `camfov` (v1's 50° by default); `terrain=phone|full`
(v1's row: the phone set stops at 4 / 20 / 128 / 384 m; captures take the full set); **M3:** v1's boat rows `settle` (s,
20), `start=x,z`, `heading`, `camdist` (hull lengths), `camyaw` (with the follow camera: the azimuth off the heading, the
chase off, as v1's hull frame), `touch=1|0` (the phone hands), `boat=0` (no boat); **M5a:** v1's deck rows `clouds=0`, `cloudcov`, `cloudtype`, `cloudbase`, `cloudthick`. v2's own rows (`v2/engine/flags.js`
V2_ROWS): `gov=0`, `maxpx` (3.4 Mpx), `dpr` (cap 2), `msaa` (4 | 1), `scale`, `w`+`h`, M2's `layers=terrain,forest,water,sky,boat`, M5b's `forest=0` (no cards: the terrain's canopy colour, M3's look)
(profiling: which scene draws run), M3's `boatdebug=1..5` (the cloth shader's terms), **M5e's** `night=0` (the old black night: no airglow, stars, band, moon glow or lights; the A/B and the budget) and `filmrate=` (a headless film's day clock). **M5e:** v1's storm rows `weather=storm`, `stormt=` (s into the arc), `stormrate=` (×), `stormref=x,z` (src/sim/weather/stormSim.js stormFromParams) and `storms=natural` (a storm may come on a summer afternoon, v1's NATURAL plan); the top bar's Weather menu (Clear | Fair | Storm) on a live page. **M3b:** `models=0` keeps the M3
procedural hull (the A/B and the budget's baseline); v1's rows `shot=` (v1's close shots: `cockpit-astern`, `transom`, `winch`,
`pedestal`, `above-behind`, `cockpit-above`, `companionway`; `v2/sim/shots.js`) and `canvas=a|b|c` (the canvas colour).
