# OpenStreetMap data (ODbL)

`data/geo/tahoe-towns.json` is a derivative database of OpenStreetMap (buildings, ski runs, roads, land use, marinas, piers and place nodes round Lake Tahoe, fetched 2026-10-29 through the Overpass API; footprints reduced to oriented boxes, lines simplified, names dropped). The files built from it keep the same terms:

- `v2/assets/night/lights.bin` (the town lights)
- `v2/assets/towns/` (`shore.vsh.gz`, `shore-phone.vsh.gz`, `mask.vtm.gz`, `mask-phone.vtm.gz`, `piers.json`, `landmarks.json`)
